Delete Your Sleekmirror Data

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Sleekmirror is operated by ABT Solutions Limited (trading as acdev). You can request deletion of your data at any time using the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Open Sleekmirror and go to Settings.
  2. Copy your Support ID (use the copy button).
  3. Email contact@acdev.app with the subject "Sleekmirror data deletion request" and your Support ID in the body.
  4. We will confirm once your data has been deleted, within 30 days of your request.

What is deleted

  • Your guest account record and credit balance.
  • All of your saved check results and their scores and feedback.
  • Any error or usage logs we can link to your Support ID.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Photos are not stored by us. They are sent to OpenAI for analysis only, and OpenAI may keep API inputs for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring under its own terms.
  • Limited records we must keep by law, if any, for up to the legally required period.
  • Anonymous, aggregated usage counts that cannot be linked back to you.

Deleting the app

Uninstalling Sleekmirror removes the app data stored on your device. Server-side records stay until you send a deletion request as above.
