Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026 | Last Updated: November 9, 2026

Sleekmirror is operated by acdev ("we," "our," or "us"). This policy explains what personal data we process when you use the Sleekmirror app or the website at sleekmirror.com (the "Service"), why we process it, and the rights you have under the GDPR and similar laws.

The controller is acdev. For privacy requests, email contact@acdev.app.

1. What we collect

  • Support ID. A device identifier, shown in Profile as your Support ID. It is also the key for your guest account, so you can use the app without creating an email account.
  • Photos, only for the request. The photo you submit for a check is held in memory long enough to send it for analysis, then discarded. We do not save the photo in our database, and we do not store it in Cloudflare R2 or any other file store.
  • Check results. The occasion you pick, the scores, the written feedback, the top fix, and the short action list. If a photo cannot be scored, we store that short reason instead of a score. We do not store the image with the result.
  • Scan balance. How many checks you have left.
  • Session data. An access token that expires after 15 minutes, and a refresh token that expires after 7 days. On the website the refresh token is an httpOnly, Secure cookie. In the app it is stored on the device. Preferences such as your default occasion stay on the device only.
  • Page analytics. Cookieless statistics from Umami, hosted by us at analytics.acdev.app. A page view can include the page address, referrer, browser, operating system, device type, and country. It is not tied to your account or Support ID. Umami does not use advertising cookies and we do not use it to build a profile of you.
  • Security logs. Our servers may record IP address, time, and request details for a short period to keep the Service secure.

The current app does not ask for your email. If you email us, we use that address only to handle your request.

2. Photos

A photo is used only to produce that check. We do not run facial recognition, we do not try to identify who is in the photo, and we do not build a face template. The analysis is instructed not to guess age, ethnicity, health, weight, religion, sexuality, or attractiveness.

Only submit a photo of yourself, or of an adult who has agreed. If the photo appears to show a minor, the check is refused and the image is not kept.

Earlier versions of Sleekmirror could store photos, including in Cloudflare R2. New checks do not. Email us with your Support ID if you want any earlier photo or account data deleted.

3. Why we process data

  • To provide the Service (contract): run the check, show your history of results, and keep your scan balance.
  • To keep accounts secure (legitimate interest): authenticate requests, limit abuse, and keep short security logs.
  • To see which pages are used (legitimate interest): the Umami stats described above. You can object by emailing us.
  • To meet a legal duty (legal obligation), if a law requires us to keep or disclose something.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who receives data

  • OpenAI. The photo and the occasion are sent to the OpenAI API to write the feedback. Under OpenAI's API terms, that content is not used to train their models. OpenAI may keep it for up to 30 days to monitor abuse. We do not keep a copy of the photo after the request.
  • MongoDB Atlas. Hosts the account record and the text of your check results.
  • Umami, on analytics.acdev.app. Receives the page analytics described above. We host this ourselves.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it. Logging out of the app ends the session on that device. It does not delete the account or the saved results.

5. Where data is processed

We and our providers may process data outside your country. OpenAI processes API requests in the United States. Where data leaves the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we rely on the provider's contractual safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, where those apply.

6. How long we keep it

  • Photos from current checks: not kept after the analysis request.
  • Check results, Support ID, and scan balance: until you ask us to delete them, or until the account is no longer needed to provide the Service.
  • Access tokens: 15 minutes. Refresh tokens: 7 days.
  • OpenAI may retain the photo it received for up to 30 days, as described above.
  • Umami page stats and security logs: only as long as needed for those purposes, then deleted or aggregated.

7. Security

Traffic uses HTTPS. Website refresh tokens are httpOnly and Secure. Photos are not written to our file storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Your rights and deletion

Email contact@acdev.app with your Support ID and we'll delete your data within 30 days. That includes your account, saved check results, and any photo still held from an earlier version of the Service. Copy the Support ID from Profile in the app.

If the GDPR applies to you, you can also ask us to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • restrict or object to processing, including analytics
  • receive a copy in a portable format, where that right applies

We may need your Support ID to find the right account. You can lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you are in the EU, that is the authority in your country of residence. You can also contact us first and we will try to resolve it.

Your results are linked to the device Support ID. If you lose, reset, or replace the device, or clear the app's storage, you may lose access and we may not be able to match the account without that ID.

9. Age

Sleekmirror is for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly keep personal data about anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used the Service, email us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date above. If a change materially affects how we use personal data, we will make that clear on this page.
