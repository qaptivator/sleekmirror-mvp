Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 4, 2026 | Last Updated: November 4, 2026

At Sleekmirror ("we," "our," or "us"), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use the Sleekmirror mobile application and web services (collectively, the "Service"). Sleekmirror is operated by acdev.

By using Sleekmirror, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide, secure, and improve our AI-powered visual analysis service:

  • Device Identifiers: An identifier for your device, collected to allow immediate app usage without forcing account creation.
  • Uploaded Images: Images you submit for analysis are uploaded to our secure object storage (Cloudflare R2).
  • AI Analysis Data: The occasion you select, plus the scores, category evaluations, and text feedback generated for each check, stored in our database.
  • Credits and Usage Data: Your daily free allowance, credit balance, and scan counts, used to apply limits.
  • Crash and Analytics Data: Crash reports and basic app usage information (such as which screens are used), collected through Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • Technical Metadata: Server logs, timestamps, IP addresses, platform details, and authentication cookies (httpOnly, Secure).
  • Email Address (Optional, when available): Email linking for cross-device recovery is not available in the current version. If we add it, we will collect your email address only if you choose to link it.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Service Delivery: To analyze uploaded images using third-party AI services and present structured feedback.
  • Account & Balance Management: To track your free daily allowance and credit balance.
  • Session Security: To authenticate user requests, prevent unauthorized access, and mitigate abusive API calls.
  • Improvement: To fix bugs and understand how the app is used.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data with trusted service providers solely to operate the Service:

  • OpenAI (GPT-4o Vision API): Images and the selected occasion are processed strictly for feedback generation. API data is not used to train OpenAI models and is retained by OpenAI for up to 30 days solely for abuse monitoring.
  • Cloudflare R2: Used for hosting uploaded images with HMAC-signed URLs (1-hour time-to-live) to restrict public exposure.
  • MongoDB Atlas: Hosted database infrastructure used to store account profiles and analysis records.
  • Google: Google Play (including billing for any purchases) and Firebase (crash reporting and analytics).

We may also disclose data if required by law.

4. Photos and Facial Data

Your photos are used only to generate your feedback. We do not use facial recognition, we do not identify people, and we do not build face templates or profiles. Only submit photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed to it.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard technical measures to safeguard your information, including httpOnly and Secure authentication cookies, encrypted HTTPS/TLS transport, signed and time-limited image links, and rate limiting. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention & Your Rights

Retention Schedule: Images and scan history are retained until you delete them. Authentication tokens expire automatically (15 minutes for access tokens, 7 days for refresh tokens).

Your Rights: You can delete any check in the app, which removes its result and its image from our storage. You can also request full data deletion at any time by emailing contact@acdev.app, and we will respond within 30 days. Deletion permanently removes your profile, scan history, and stored images. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, or object to the processing of your personal data.

Device-based access: Because the current version has no sign-up, your data is linked to your device. If you lose, reset, or replace your device, or uninstall the app, you may lose access to your data and we may not be able to recover it.

7. Age Requirement

Sleekmirror is for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used the Service, contact us and we will delete the data.

8. International Processing

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
